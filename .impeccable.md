## Design Context

### Users
HD2QD / Helldivers 2 players who open the lobby browser inside the Quick Deploy manager to find and join a public lobby. They scan a dense table, compare a few rows, then act with the Join button. The UI must stay readable at a glance and never hide the primary action.

### Brand Personality
Industrial, utilitarian, game-console. Concise and confident; no decorative flourishes.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the HD2QD manager theme exactly: `Ground900`/`Ground700`, `Grid`, `Ink`, `Muted`, `Yellow`, `Blue`; Ghost/Primary/Chip/Tick controls; no custom palette, no separate visual language. The plugin is a tab, so it reuses the manager's ground and acrylic texture rather than painting its own.

### Design Principles
- Reuse manager tokens and components; never introduce a parallel theme.
- Structure with proximity: status and progress detail are one information stack; Join is the single primary action at its right.
- The bottom edge is a stable frame: content keeps its breathing room whether or not progress is visible; the progress track bleeds to the edge without stealing layout height.
- Motion explains state only: determinate progress eases toward its target; indeterminate motion enters from the left and exits to the right, never bouncing at the edge.
- Respect reduced-motion settings: keep information legible with motion simplified.
